# 📘 《走遍美国》第11集 深度学习讲义

**课文主题：** Marilyn 与 Richard 计划买房，向 Ellen 与房产经纪 Mrs. Martinelli 咨询，并去银行问房贷。
**核心场景：** 向 Ellen 征求买房意见 → 与房产经纪看房 → 银行问抵押贷款的担保问题。
**学习重点：** 房产业术语（mortgage, real-estate agent, down payment）、闪音（better, water, photographer）、条件句与建议句型。

---

## 发音标注图例（Pronunciation Legend）

| 符号 | 名称 | 说明 | 示例 |
| ------- | -------------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| ⌒ | 连读 Linking | 前字尾辅音与后字首元音拼读，或元元连读 | `a⌒lot`, `go⌒on` |
| (schwa) | 弱读 Weak Form | 结构词元音弱化为 /ə/ 或 /ɪ/ | `t(schwa)` /tə/, `of(schwa)` /əv/ |
| `[ ]` | 失去爆破 Unreleased Stop | /p,t,k,b,d,g/ 只作发音姿势，不送气爆破 | `no[t] problem`, `don'[t]` |
| ⚡ | 闪音 Flap T/D | 两元音间 /t/、/d/ 快速弹舌，似轻快 /d/ | `wa⚡er`, `ci⚡y`, `pho⚡ographer` |

## 🔬 第四部分：重要语法讲解（Grammar）

### 1. "with + 名词 + 现在分词" 伴随结构

**课文例句：**
We feel that with a baby coming we need to have our own place.

**结构：**
with + 名词 + V-ing，表示伴随状况或背景。

**用法讲解：**
with 复合结构说明伴随情境。剧中用 "with a baby coming" 交代背景。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| with time passing | 随时间流逝 | With time passing, we decide. |
| with kids growing | 孩子长大 | With kids growing, we need space. |
| without help | 没有帮助 | We did it without help. |

**易错点：**
with 后接代词用宾格（with him / with me），不接主格。

**总结：**
with 复合结构让句子更紧凑，适合描述背景。

### 2. "could / couldn't + 动词原形" 能力/过去

**课文例句：**
We couldn't afford it. Grandpa wanted to lend us the money.

**结构：**
can 的过去式 could；否定 couldn't + 原形。

**用法讲解：**
could 表过去能力或委婉可能。剧中说明过去"负担不起"。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| We could try. | 我们可以试 | We could try the bank. |
| She could help. | 她能帮忙 | She could help us. |
| He couldn't come. | 他来不了 | He couldn't come then. |

**易错点：**
could 后接原形；表过去用 couldn't，非 can't。

**总结：**
could 表过去能力最常用，注意与时态一致。

### 3. "Would like to..." 意愿

**课文例句：**
We'd like to discuss a mortgage. / We'd like to find out about a mortgage.

**结构：**
would like (sb.) to + 原形；'d like 为缩写。

**用法讲解：**
礼貌表达愿望，比 want 得体，常用于正式/服务场景。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| I'd like to see. | 我想看 | I'd like to see the house. |
| We'd like to know. | 我们想知道 | We'd like to know the price. |
| She'd like a loan. | 她想要贷款 | She'd like a loan. |

**易错点：**
would like 后接 to do，不接 doing。

**总结：**
'd like 是银行、购物、预约场景的礼貌核心句。

### 4. "If" 条件句（真实条件）

**课文例句：**
If we buy a house, I want to be able to handle it alone.

**结构：**
If + 一般现在时，主句用will/现在时（真实条件）。

**用法讲解：**
if 引导条件，"如果…就…"。剧中 Richard 表达独立心愿。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| If we save, we buy. | 如果存钱就买 | If we save, we can buy. |
| If it's cheap, take it. | 若便宜就买 | If it's a bargain, take it. |
| When we move... | 当我们搬… | When we move, call me. |

**易错点：**
真实条件主句不用 will 在 if 从句（误：If we will buy）。

**总结：**
if 条件句是商量计划的高频结构，注意时态搭配。

### 5. "be prepared to..." 准备做

**课文例句：**
Are you prepared to make a ten-percent down payment?

**结构：**
be prepared to + 动词原形。

**用法讲解：**
表"准备好做某事"，比 ready 稍正式，常用于手续/承诺。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| ready to sign | 准备好签字 | I'm ready to sign. |
| willing to pay | 愿意付 | We're willing to pay. |
| able to afford | 能负担 | We're able to afford it. |

**易错点：**
prepared 后接 to do，不接 doing。

**总结：**
be prepared to 在签约、贷款场景很地道。

### 6. "have something to do" 有…要做

**课文例句：**
We've got a lot to talk about. / Someone to sign for the loan.

**结构：**
have + 名词 + to do，不定式作定语。

**用法讲解：**
不定式修饰前面的名词，表示"要去做的"。剧中 "a lot to talk about"。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| much to learn | 很多要学 | We have much to learn. |
| a house to buy | 要买的房 | We have a house to buy. |
| nobody to ask | 无人可问 | We have nobody to ask. |

**易错点：**
此结构 to do 主动表被动含义，不写 to be done。

**总结：**
have + 名 + to do 让表达简洁，适合列举事务。

## 🌱 第五部分：知识点拓展（Expansion）

### 从 `a real-estate agent` 拓展：房屋相关职业

**课文原句：**
Maybe we should speak to a real-estate agent about a house.

**中文意思：**
也许我们该和房产经纪谈谈房子。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| real-estate agent | 房产经纪人 | Call a real-estate agent. |
| realtor | 房地产经纪人 | A realtor showed us homes. |
| loan officer | 贷款专员 | Talk to the loan officer. |
| broker | 中介/经纪 | The broker found a buyer. |

**总结：**
美国买房常通过 real-estate agent / realtor；贷款找 loan officer。这些词在置业对话中必备。

### 从 `down payment` 拓展：买房财务词

**课文原句：**
Are you prepared to make a ten-percent down payment?

**中文意思：**
你准备付一成首付吗？

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| down payment | 首付 | We made a 10% down payment. |
| mortgage | 按揭 | We need a mortgage. |
| collateral | 抵押品 | We have no collateral. |
| monthly payment | 月供 | The monthly payment is low. |

**总结：**
置业核心财务词：down payment（首付）、mortgage（按揭）、monthly payment（月供）、collateral（抵押）。连读时注意⌒连接。

### 从 `It's a bargain` 拓展：评价价格

**课文原句：**
Here's a real buy. It's a bargain.

**中文意思：**
这是真正值得买的，很划算。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| It's a bargain. | 很划算 | This house is a bargain. |
| a real buy | 真划算 | That's a real buy. |
| good value | 性价比高 | It's good value. |
| overpriced | 太贵 | That one is overpriced. |

**总结：**
bargain / a real buy 表"划算"；overpriced 相反。看房时可用来评价。

### 从 `independent` 拓展：性格描述

**课文原句：**
He's independent, and sometimes just stubborn.

**中文意思：**
他很独立，有时也挺固执。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| independent | 独立的 | He's independent. |
| stubborn | 固执的 | She's a bit stubborn. |
| determined | 坚定的 | He's determined. |
| easygoing | 随和的 | My dad is easygoing. |

**总结：**
描述家人性格时，independent 与 stubborn 常成对出现；determined 偏褒义。

### 从 `As a matter of fact` 拓展：补充强调

**课文原句：**
As a matter of fact, my friend Virginia Martinelli is a real-estate agent.

**中文意思：**
事实上，我朋友 Virginia 就是房产经纪。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| As a matter of fact | 事实上 | As a matter of fact, I know him. |
| In fact | 实际上 | In fact, it's true. |
| Actually | 其实 | Actually, I agree. |
| To tell the truth | 说实话 | To tell the truth, I'm tired. |

**总结：**
补充或纠正时用 As a matter of fact / In fact；actually 最口语。

### 从 `have a point` 拓展：认同观点

**课文原句：**
Richard has a point. You're just beginning to look.

**中文意思：**
Richard 说得有道理，你们才刚开始看。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| have a point | 有道理 | You have a point there. |
| make sense | 说得通 | That makes sense. |
| I see your point | 我懂你的意思 | I see your point. |
| fair enough | 有道理 | Fair enough, let's wait. |

**总结：**
认同对方时，have a point 自然得体；make sense 更通用。

### 从 `the minute he comes home` 拓展：时间连接

**课文原句：**
I'll talk to Richard about it the minute he comes home.

**中文意思：**
他一回家我就跟 Richard 说。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| the minute... | 一…就 | I'll call the minute he's here. |
| as soon as | 一…就 | As soon as he comes, tell him. |
| the moment... | 一…就 | The moment she arrives, we leave. |
| once... | 一旦 | Once we save, we buy. |

**总结：**
the minute / as soon as / once 都表"一…就"，口语常用 the minute 更生动。
