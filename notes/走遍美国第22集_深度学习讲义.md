# 📘 《走遍美国》第22集 深度学习讲义

**课文主题：** Marilyn 在"重返时装设计工作"与"全职照顾儿子 Max"之间犹豫，最终决定在家为精品店设计婚纱，两全其美。
**核心场景：** 卧室哄娃时与 Richard 谈职业抉择；与 Ellen、Susan 讨论职场妈妈的经验；Rita Mae 来访敲定在家创业方案。
**学习重点：** ⌒ 连读（如 go⌒on）、`(schwa)` 弱读（to/of/and）、`[ ]` 失去爆破（don'[t]）、⚡ 闪音（be⚡er / wa⚡er）；职场与家庭平衡相关词汇；whether 从句、现在完成进行时、why can't you... 建议句型。

## 发音标注图例（Pronunciation Legend）

| 符号 | 名称 | 说明 | 示例 |
| ------- | -------------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| ⌒ | 连读 Linking | 前字尾辅音与后字首元音拼读，或元元连读 | `a⌒lot`, `go⌒on` |
| (schwa) | 弱读 Weak Form | 结构词元音弱化为 /ə/ 或 /ɪ/ | `t(schwa)` /tə/, `of(schwa)` /əv/ |
| `[ ]` | 失去爆破 Unreleased Stop | /p,t,k,b,d,g/ 只作发音姿势，不送气爆破 | `no[t] problem`, `don'[t]` |
| ⚡ | 闪音 Flap T/D | 两元音间 /t/、/d/ 快速弹舌，似轻快 /d/ | `wa⚡er`, `ci⚡y`, `pho⚡ographer` |

> **文本高亮约定：** **加粗** = 生词；

## 🔬 第四部分：重要语法讲解（Grammar）

### 1. 现在完成进行时（have been + V-ing）

**课文例句：**
I've been thinking a lot about our responsibilities.

**结构：**
have/has been + 现在分词，强调动作从过去持续到现在、仍在进行。

**用法讲解：**
表示一段时间以来持续的活动，常带 for/since 或隐含"近来一直在"。比一般现在时更突出"过程感"。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| I have thought about it. | 我已想过（结果） | I have thought about the plan. |
| I was thinking... | 我当时在想 | I was thinking of you. |
| I think about it often. | 我经常想 | I think about it every day. |

**易错点：**
易与现在完成时混淆；若强调"一直持续、未完成"，用进行时更贴切。

**总结：**
描述"近来一直在纠结/考虑"时，优先用 have been thinking / wrestling with，强调过程。

### 2. whether 引导的名词性从句

**课文例句：**
I've been wrestling with the question of whether I go back to work or not.

**结构：**
whether + 主语 + 谓语（可接 or not），作介词/动词的宾语。

**用法讲解：**
whether 表示"是否"，引导从句充当名词成分；or not 可省略或后置。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| if I should... | 是否该… | I wonder if I should go. |
| whether to... | 是否做…（不定式） | I can't decide whether to stay. |
| the question of... | …的问题 | the question of career choice |

**易错点：**
介词后一般只用 whether，不用 if（of whether 正确，of if 错误）。

**总结：**
在 about / of 等介词后引"是否"，务必用 whether。

### 3. Why can't you...? 委婉建议

**课文例句：**
Why can't you work at home, Marilyn?

**结构：**
Why can't + 主语 + 动词原形？用反问形式提出建议。

**用法讲解：**
字面问"为什么不能"，实际是"你应该/可以…吧"，比直接建议更柔和、引发对方思考。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Why don't you...? | 你为什么不…？ | Why don't you try it? |
| You could... | 你可以… | You could design at home. |
| How about...? | …怎么样？ | How about working at home? |

**易错点：**
这是建议而非真问原因，回答时不要认真解释"为什么不能"。

**总结：**
用 Why can't you...? 提建议，语气自然、鼓励性强，适合朋友间。

### 4. if 引导的非真实条件句（与现在事实相反）

**课文例句：**
If I had a child as cute as Max, I might do the same thing.

**结构：**
If + 过去时（be 多用 were），主句用 would/could/might + 动词原形。

**用法讲解：**
表达与现在事实相反的假设（Rita Mae 实际没有孩子），语气委婉、带想象色彩。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| If I were you,... | 如果我是你 | If I were you, I'd stay home. |
| I would... if... | 我会…如果… | I might help if I had time. |
| Suppose... | 假设… | Suppose you had a baby. |

**易错点：**
从句用过去时表"非真实"，不是过去发生的事；口语中 if I was 也常见但 were 更规范。

**总结：**
表达"如果（实际上不是）…我就…"，用 if + 过去时 / would(might)。

### 5. as... as 同级比较

**课文例句：**
If I had a child as cute as Max...

**结构：**
as + 形容词/副词原级 + as，表示"和…一样"。

**用法讲解：**
否定可用 not so/as...as；本句用 as cute as 强调程度相当。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| so...as（否定） | 不如… | not so cute as Max |
| the same as | 和…相同 | the same as before |
| like + 名词 | 像… | a child like Max |

**易错点：**
肯定句一般用 as...as，不用 so...as；as 后接名词时注意语序 as + 形容词 + a/an + 名词。

**总结：**
as cute as Max 中形容词夹在 as…as 之间，注意 "as + adj. + a + noun" 结构。

### 6. 动名词作主语/表语

**课文例句：**
Designing dresses is a career you could establish out of your home.

**结构：**
V-ing 短语作主语，谓语动词用单数。

**用法讲解：**
把"做某事"当作一件事来陈述，突出活动本身。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| To design... | 设计…（不定式） | To design at home is possible. |
| It is... to... | 做…是… | It is good to design at home. |
| Noun phrase | 名词短语 | Dress design is a career. |

**易错点：**
动名词主语视为单数，勿用复数动词。

**总结：**
用 Designing/V-ing 起头总括一种活动，适合定义式表达。

| I'm torn between A and B. | 在A与B间纠结 | I'm torn between work and family. |
| It's a tough call. | 这是个难做的决定 | Quitting is a tough call. |
| I can't make up my mind. | 我做不了决定 | I can't make up my mind yet. |

**总结：**
I'm torn 最口语、最形象；torn between A and B 点明两难对象。描述内心矛盾时，这几个表达可互换，注意 tough call 偏美式口语。

### 从 `wrestling with` 拓展：与"困难"搏斗的动词

**课文原句：**
I've been wrestling with the question of whether I go back to work or not.

**中文意思：**
我一直在苦苦思考/纠结这个问题。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| wrestle with | 奋力对付（难题） | He's wrestling with a problem. |
| grapple with | 努力解决 | We must grapple with the issue. |
| struggle with | 挣扎于 | She struggles with doubt. |
| cope with | 应付 | I can cope with the pressure. |

**总结：**
wrestle/grapple 比 struggle 更显"主动用力"；cope with 侧重"扛得住"。作写作与口语升级词很实用。

### 从 `You shouldn't have` 拓展：收礼/受惠的客套

**课文原句：**
Oh, it's beautiful, Rita Mae! You shouldn't have.

**中文意思：**
你太客气了/真不该破费。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| You shouldn't have. | 你不必破费 | What a gift! You shouldn't have. |
| That's very kind of you. | 你真好 | That's very kind of you. |
| I owe you one. | 我欠你人情 | Thanks, I owe you one. |
| Don't mention it. | 不足挂齿（回应谢） | Thank you! — Don't mention it. |

**总结：**
You shouldn't have 是收到礼物时标准客套，体现谦逊；回应感谢则用 Don't mention it / My pleasure。

### 从 `I'm all ears` 拓展：关于"倾听"

**课文原句：**
You sounded like you've made a decision... I'm all ears.

**中文意思：**
我洗耳恭听。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| I'm all ears. | 我专心听 | Tell me the news — I'm all ears. |
| Listen up. | 听好了（提醒） | Listen up, everyone. |
| I'm listening. | 我在听 | Go ahead, I'm listening. |
| Hear me out. | 听我把话说完 | Please hear me out. |

**总结：**
I'm all ears 俏皮且热情；hear me out 强调"别打断"。正式场合用 I'm listening 更稳重。

### 从 `wound up` 拓展：情绪/状态形容词短语

**课文原句：**
I've never seen you so wound up.

**中文意思：**
我从未见你这么紧张焦虑。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| wound up | 焦虑紧绷 | He's really wound up today. |
| stressed out | 压力大 | She's stressed out at work. |
| on edge | 坐立不安 | I feel on edge before exams. |
| keyed up | 兴奋/紧张 | The team is keyed up. |

**总结：**
wound up / stressed out / on edge 近义，描述不同程度的紧张；keyed up 偏"既兴奋又紧张"。

### 从 `benefit financially` 拓展：获益表达

**课文原句：**
And you can both benefit financially.

**中文意思：**
你们双方都能在财务上获益。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| benefit from | 从…获益 | We benefit from the plan. |
| gain from | 从…得到 | He gained from the deal. |
| profit from | 从…获利 | The company profited from it. |
| reap the rewards | 收获回报 | They reaped the rewards. |

**总结：**
benefit 最通用、中性；profit 偏金钱；reap the rewards 更书面。副词 financially 可替换为 greatly / mutually。

### 从 `custom-designed` 拓展："定制"相关词族

**课文原句：**
Do you remember our talks about custom-designed dresses for the boutique?

**中文意思：**
还记得我们谈过为精品店做定制礼服吗？

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| custom-made | 定制的 | a custom-made suit |
| tailor-made | 量身定做的 | a tailor-made plan |
| made-to-order | 按单定做 | made-to-order shoes |
| off-the-rack | 现成的（反义） | off-the-rack clothes |

**总结：**
custom-/tailor-made 强调专属定制；off-the-rack 是其反义，购物与商务场景都常用。

### 从 `expand` 拓展：发展业务的动词

**课文原句：**
If it's successful, we can expand to all kinds of dresses.

**中文意思：**
若成功，可拓展到各类礼服。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| expand | 扩张 | expand the market |
| grow | 成长 | grow the business |
| scale up | 扩大规模 | scale up production |
| branch out | 拓展新领域 | branch out into new areas |

**总结：**
expand 最常用；branch out 强调"横向拓新业务"；scale up 偏运营规模。创业者口语必备。

## ❓ 第六部分：学习问题（Study Questions）

**Comprehension（理解）**
1. Why was Marilyn "torn" between going back to work and staying home?（为何 Marilyn 左右为难？）
2. What solution did Susan suggest so Marilyn could keep both her career and motherhood?（Susan 提出了什么两全方案？）
3. What kind of dresses did Marilyn decide to design at home, and who was her first customer?（她决定在家设计什么？首位客户是谁？）

**Thinking & Speaking（思考与口语）**
4. If you were Marilyn, would you choose full-time work, full-time motherhood, or both? Why?（若是你，会怎么选？）
5. Do you think working from home is a good idea for new parents? Give reasons.（在家工作对新手父母好吗？）
6. How would you persuade your boss to accept a work-from-home arrangement?（如何说服老板接受居家办公？）

**Retelling（复述）**
7. Retell Act I in 3–4 sentences: how Marilyn first raised her dilemma with Richard.（复述第一幕：Marilyn 如何向 Richard 提出两难。）
8. Retell Act III: how Marilyn presented her "two-half" decision to Rita Mae and what they agreed on.（复述第三幕：她如何向 Rita Mae 说明决定及共识。）
9. Suppose you are Rita Mae. Summarize the home-based wedding-dress plan to a friend in your own words.（以 Rita Mae 口吻向朋友转述该计划。）
10. Use the words boutique, career, decision, custom-designed, benefit to retell the story in one paragraph.（用所给词一段话复述全剧。）
