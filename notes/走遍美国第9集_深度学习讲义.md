# 📘 《走遍美国》第9集 深度学习讲义

**课文主题：** Robbie 面临毕业去向，父亲 Philip 安排密歇根大学面试，但他想学新闻、自己做决定。
**核心场景：** 深夜父子谈毕业感受 → 密歇根大学面试 → Robbie 向朋友与父亲说出想学新闻的愿望。
**学习重点：** 弱读 (schwa)、闪音（better, writer, water）、表达不确定的 reservations / uncertain、make one's own decision。

---

## 发音标注图例（Pronunciation Legend）

| 符号 | 名称 | 说明 | 示例 |
| ------- | -------------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| ⌒ | 连读 Linking | 前字尾辅音与后字首元音拼读，或元元连读 | `a⌒lot`, `go⌒on` |
| (schwa) | 弱读 Weak Form | 结构词元音弱化为 /ə/ 或 /ɪ/ | `t(schwa)` /tə/, `of(schwa)` /əv/ |
| `[ ]` | 失去爆破 Unreleased Stop | /p,t,k,b,d,g/ 只作发音姿势，不送气爆破 | `no[t] problem`, `don'[t]` |
| ⚡ | 闪音 Flap T/D | 两元音间 /t/、/d/ 快速弹舌，似轻快 /d/ | `wa⚡er`, `ci⚡y`, `pho⚡ographer` |

> **文本高亮约定：** **加粗** = 生词；

## 🔬 第四部分：重要语法讲解（Grammar）

### 1. have to / has to 表"必须"

**课文例句：**
I have to turn it in in the morning.

**结构：**
主语 + have/has to + 动词原形（第三人称用 has to）。

**用法讲解：**
have to 表示客观必须，比 must 更口语化。本句说明 Robbie 客观上交稿截止。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| I must go. | 我必须走 | I must leave now. |
| She has to study. | 她得学习 | She has to study tonight. |
| We need to eat. | 我们需要吃 | We need to eat first. |

**易错点：**
第三人称用 has to（误：he have to）。

**总结：**
口语中 have to 比 must 更常用，注意三单变化。

### 2. 动名词作宾语（介词后）

**课文例句：**
I'm writing an article on the feelings about graduation.

**结构：**
介词 + V-ing；或动词（如 enjoy, mind, suggest）+ V-ing。

**用法讲解：**
about, in, of 等介词后接动词须用 -ing。本集多处出现 "about going / thinking of becoming"。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| think about going | 考虑去 | He thought about going. |
| enjoy writing | 喜欢写 | I enjoy writing. |
| thank you for coming | 谢你来 | Thanks for coming. |

**易错点：**
介词后不可用原形（误：about go）。

**总结：**
见到介词就想 -ing，是口语语法高频点。

### 3. "too + adj. + to do" 太…而不能

**课文例句：**
It's not easy, and this is an important decision we have to make.

**结构：**
too + 形容词/副词 + to + 动词原形（本集虽未直接出现 too...to，但 "not easy" 相关）。

**用法讲解：**
补充：too...to 表"过于…以致不能"。可用 "not easy to decide" 表达类似否定。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| too tired to walk | 太累走不动 | He was too tired to walk. |
| too young to drive | 太小不能开车 | She's too young to drive. |
| so hard we can't stop | 太难停不下来 | It's so hard we can't stop. |

**易错点：**
too...to 本身含否定，不再加 not。

**总结：**
too...to 是表达"过度导致不能"的紧凑结构。

### 4. "Would like to..." 委婉愿望

**课文例句：**
My father would like me to go there.

**结构：**
would like (sb.) to + 动词原形；口语缩写为 'd like。

**用法讲解：**
比 want 更礼貌委婉，用于表达希望。Robbie 转述父亲愿望。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| I'd like to go. | 我想去 | I'd like to go there. |
| She'd like a coffee. | 她想喝咖啡 | She'd like some coffee. |
| We'd like to help. | 我们想帮忙 | We'd like to help you. |

**易错点：**
would like 后接名词或 to do，不接 doing。

**总结：**
'd like 是日常提出愿望、请求最得体的表达。

### 5. 现在完成时 have + 过去分词

**课文例句：**
You've been writing for the school paper. / I've thought a lot about which college.

**结构：**
have/has + 过去分词，强调过去动作与现在的联系。

**用法讲解：**
完成时连接过去与现在。Dean 看到 Robbie "have been writing" 的持续经历。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| I've finished. | 我做完了 | I've finished the paper. |
| She has left. | 她走了 | She has left already. |
| We've decided. | 我们决定了 | We've decided at last. |

**易错点：**
不规则动词过去分词易错（write→written，非 writed）。

**总结：**
完成时让叙述更有"延续到此刻"的语感。

### 6. "Why don't we...?" 提建议

**课文例句：**
So why don't we get right to work?

**结构：**
Why don't + 主语 + 动词原形？表建议。

**用法讲解：**
比 "Let's" 稍委婉，常含"我们何不…"。Dean 用其推进面试。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Why not go? | 何不去？ | Why not join us? |
| Let's start. | 我们开始吧 | Let's start now. |
| How about a break? | 休息下？ | How about a short break? |

**易错点：**
don't 后接原形，不接 to do。

**总结：**
Why don't we 是提出建议、推动行动的自然句式。

## 🌱 第五部分：知识点拓展（Expansion）

### 从 `make my own decision` 拓展：独立与决定

**课文原句：**
Growing up means making my own decisions, doesn't it?

**中文意思：**
长大意味着自己做决定，不是吗？

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| make my own decision | 自己做决定 | I want to make my own decision. |
| decide for myself | 为自己决定 | I can decide for myself. |
| be my own boss | 自己做主 | I'd like to be my own boss. |
| take charge | 负起责任 | It's time to take charge. |

**总结：**
表达"独立"时，make one's own decision 最正式通用；be my own boss 更口语生动。配合 "doesn't it?" 反问很自然。

### 从 `have some reservations` 拓展：犹豫与不确定

**课文原句：**
You seem to have some reservations.

**中文意思：**
你似乎有所保留/犹豫。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| have reservations | 有保留 | I have some reservations. |
| have doubts | 有怀疑 | I have doubts about it. |
| be uncertain | 不确定 | I'm uncertain about the plan. |
| be hesitant | 犹豫的 | She's hesitant to say yes. |

**总结：**
reservations 较正式；doubts 更口语；uncertain 直接描述状态。面试/商量场景常用。

### 从 `follow in one's footsteps` 拓展：继承与效仿

**课文原句：**
You can follow in your father's footsteps.

**中文意思：**
你可以步你父亲的后尘（继承他的路）。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| follow in one's footsteps | 继承某人事业 | He followed in his dad's footsteps. |
| take after someone | 像（长辈） | She takes after her mother. |
| carry on the tradition | 延续传统 | They carry on the family tradition. |
| model after | 以…为榜样 | I model myself after him. |

**总结：**
follow in one's footsteps 强调职业/道路传承；take after 偏外貌性格。

### 从 `hear me out` 拓展：倾听与沟通

**课文原句：**
Thanks for hearing me out.

**中文意思：**
谢谢你听我把话说完。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| hear someone out | 听人说完 | Please hear me out. |
| listen to me | 听我说 | Just listen to me. |
| let me finish | 让我说完 | Let me finish, please. |
| pay attention | 注意听 | Pay attention to this. |

**总结：**
hear out 强调"听完整个观点"，沟通中很体贴；比 listen 更完整。

### 从 `It doesn't mean...` 拓展：澄清与转折

**课文原句：**
It doesn't mean you're going to Michigan.

**中文意思：**
这不代表你就要去密歇根。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| It doesn't mean... | 这不代表… | It doesn't mean you failed. |
| That's not to say... | 并非说… | That's not to say I agree. |
| I don't mean to... | 我不是要… | I don't mean to upset you. |
| It's just that... | 只是… | It's just that I'm busy. |

**总结：**
澄清意图时，It doesn't mean 避免误会；配合 but 引出真正意思。

### 从 `impressed with` 拓展：赞赏与印象

**课文原句：**
He told me he was very impressed with you.

**中文意思：**
他告诉我他对你印象很深。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| impressed with | 对…印象深刻 | I'm impressed with your work. |
| amazed by | 惊叹于 | I'm amazed by her talent. |
| struck by | 被…打动 | I was struck by his kindness. |
| in awe of | 钦佩 | They're in awe of the teacher. |

**总结：**
impressed with 最常用；amazed by 程度更强；struck by 偏书面。

### 从 `a piece of advice` 拓展：给建议

**课文原句：**
One piece of advice. The most important thing is for you to decide your own future.

**中文意思：**
一条建议。最重要的是你自己决定未来。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| a piece of advice | 一条建议 | Let me give you a piece of advice. |
| a word of warning | 一句提醒 | A word of warning: be careful. |
| my two cents | 我的浅见 | Here's my two cents. |
| if I were you | 如果我是你 | If I were you, I'd wait. |

**总结：**
a piece of advice 正式；my two cents 随意亲切；if I were you 用于假设建议。
