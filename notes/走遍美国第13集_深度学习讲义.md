# 📘 《走遍美国》第13集 深度学习讲义

**课文主题：** 全家迎接新生儿 Max 回家，回顾 Stewart 家族传统与亲情，讲述爷爷的棒球手套与 TLC。
**核心场景：** 布置欢迎房间 → Max 回家众人迎接 → 讲述家族传统与婴儿用品 → 亲友探望与照料。
**学习重点：** 闪音（better, water, writer, photographer）、家庭亲情语汇、a real Stewart / TLC 等文化表达。

## 发音标注图例（Pronunciation Legend）

| 符号 | 名称 | 说明 | 示例 |
| ------- | -------------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| ⌒ | 连读 Linking | 前字尾辅音与后字首元音拼读，或元元连读 | `a⌒lot`, `go⌒on` |
| (schwa) | 弱读 Weak Form | 结构词元音弱化为 /ə/ 或 /ɪ/ | `t(schwa)` /tə/, `of(schwa)` /əv/ |
| `[ ]` | 失去爆破 Unreleased Stop | /p,t,k,b,d,g/ 只作发音姿势，不送气爆破 | `no[t] problem`, `don'[t]` |
| ⚡ | 闪音 Flap T/D | 两元音间 /t/、/d/ 快速弹舌，似轻快 /d/ | `wa⚡er`, `ci⚡y`, `pho⚡ographer` |

## 🔬 第四部分：重要语法讲解（Grammar）

### 1. "there + be" 存在句

**课文例句：**
There's nothing more joyous than the arrival of a new baby.

**结构：**
There is / are + 名词，表"有…"。

**用法讲解：**
there be 说明存在。剧中 Ellen 感叹"没有什么比新生儿到来更欢乐"。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| There is a boy. | 有个男孩 | There is a boy named Max. |
| There are gifts. | 有礼物 | There are many gifts. |
| Is there a room? | 有房间吗 | Is there a room ready? |

**易错点：**
be 动词单复数随最近名词（there is a boy / there are boys）。

**总结：**
there be 是描述"存在/拥有"最基础句型。

### 2. "so + 形容词/副词 + that" 结果

**课文例句：**
If he talks as loudly as he cries, we're all in for trouble.

**结构：**
so + adj./adv. + that 从句（本集以 as...as 比较体现类似逻辑）。

**用法讲解：**
补充：so...that 表"如此…以至于"。剧中用 as loudly as 比较，强调 Max 哭声大。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| so big that... | 大到… | He's so big that we worry. |
| as cute as... | 和…一样可爱 | He's as cute as Max. |
| such a boy that... | 这样的男孩 | He's such a boy that we laugh. |

**易错点：**
so 后接形容词/副词，such 后接名词。

**总结：**
so / such / as...as 都是比较与程度的核心结构。

### 3. "used to + 名词" 习惯于

**课文例句：**
A baby gets used to voices.

**结构：**
get / be used to + 名词或 V-ing，表"习惯于"。

**用法讲解：**
注意与 used to do（过去习惯）不同：be/get used to 后接名词或 -ing，表"适应"。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| get used to noise | 习惯噪音 | The baby gets used to noise. |
| be used to city | 习惯城市 | He is used to the city. |
| accustomed to | 习惯于 | She's accustomed to it. |

**易错点：**
used to 后接 -ing（误：used to live → 表习惯；get used to live ❌ → living）。

**总结：**
区分 used to do（过去常）与 be/get used to doing（习惯于）。

### 4. "would you like to...?" 礼貌邀请

**课文例句：**
Would you like to see baby Max, Michelle?

**结构：**
Would you like to + 原形？表礼貌邀请/意愿。

**用法讲解：**
比 do you want 更客气，适合邀请晚辈/客人。剧中 Susan 邀 Michelle 看宝宝。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| Do you want to...? | 你想…？ | Do you want to see him? |
| Care to...? | 愿意…？ | Care to join us? |
| How about...? | …如何？ | How about seeing Max? |

**易错点：**
would you like 后接 to do，不接 doing。

**总结：**
would you like to 是邀请与提议的得体句式。

### 5. "can count on it" 确信

**课文例句：**
You can count on it.

**结构：**
can count on + 名词/代词，表"可以指望/确信"。

**用法讲解：**
count on 表依赖、确信。Robbie 用其肯定 Susan 与 Harry 会结婚。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| depend on it | 信赖它 | You can depend on it. |
| be sure | 确定 | You can be sure. |
| bet on it | 打赌确定 | You can bet on it. |

**易错点：**
count on 后接名词/代词/动名词（误：count on do）。

**总结：**
count on it 是口语中表达"绝对可信"的轻松说法。

### 6. 反意疑问句

**课文例句：**
Grandpa really loves his family, doesn't he?

**结构：**
陈述句 + 助动词 + not + 主语？前肯后否。

**用法讲解：**
反意疑问用于确认。剧中 Marilyn 确认 Grandpa 爱家人。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| He's tall, isn't he? | 他高，不是吗 | He's tall, isn't he? |
| They left, didn't they? | 他们走了，对吧 | They left, didn't they? |
| You like it, don't you? | 你喜欢，是吧 | You like it, don't you? |

**易错点：**
前肯后否、前否后肯；时态与主语一致。

**总结：**
反意疑问让对话更自然，适合确认与互动。

## 🌱 第五部分：知识点拓展（Expansion）

### 从 `a real Stewart` 拓展：家族身份

**课文原句：**
Max looks just like Grandpa. — A real Stewart.

**中文意思：**
Max 真像爷爷。——真正的 Stewart 家人。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| a real Stewart | 真正的家人 | He's a real Stewart. |
| one of the family | 自家人 | She's one of the family. |
| blood relative | 血亲 | He's a blood relative. |
| part of the clan | 家族一员 | Welcome, part of the clan. |

**总结：**
a real Stewart 带有自豪与归属感；one of the family 更通用。描述家族认同时选用。

### 从 `TLC` 拓展：关爱表达

**课文原句：**
To be with our family and all that Stewart TLC.

**中文意思：**
和家人在一起，还有 Stewart 家那份体贴关爱。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| TLC (tender loving care) | 体贴关爱 | The baby needs TLC. |
| love and care | 爱与关怀 | They gave love and care. |
| tender loving care | 温柔照料 | She showed tender loving care. |
| warm affection | 温暖亲情 | We felt warm affection. |

**总结：**
TLC 是 tender loving care 的首字母缩写，口语常用；love and care 更直白。

### 从 `I'm fond of her` 拓展：表达喜爱

**课文原句：**
I'm very fond of her.

**中文意思：**
我很喜欢她。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| be fond of | 喜欢 | I'm fond of him. |
| care for | 在乎/喜欢 | I care for her. |
| be attached to | 依恋 | She's attached to the baby. |
| have a soft spot for | 偏爱 | I have a soft spot for Max. |

**总结：**
be fond of 比 like 温柔；have a soft spot for 更显偏爱。亲情场景常用。

### 从 `take after` 拓展：像长辈

**课文原句：**
I take after my mother's family.

**中文意思：**
我像我母亲那边的家人。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| take after | 像（长辈） | He takes after his dad. |
| look like | 长得像 | She looks like her mom. |
| be the spitting image of | 一模一样 | He's the spitting image of Grandpa. |
| favor (US) | 像 | The boy favors his father. |

**总结：**
take after 偏性格/外貌；look like 仅外貌；spitting image 强调极像。

### 从 `get along (well)` 拓展：相处

**课文原句：**
They get along so well.

**中文意思：**
他们相处得很好。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| get along (well) | 相处（好） | They get along well. |
| get on (well) | 相处（好） | We get on fine. |
| hit it off | 一拍即合 | They hit it off at once. |
| be close | 亲密 | The family is close. |

**总结：**
get along / get on 同义；hit it off 强调初见投缘。描述关系时常用。

### 从 `pick it out` 拓展：挑选

**课文原句：**
Michelle picked it out.

**中文意思：**
Michelle 把它挑出来的。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| pick out | 挑出 | She picked out the blue one. |
| choose | 选择 | I chose this gift. |
| select | 精选 | He selected a book. |
| go for | 选中 | I'll go for the red one. |

**总结：**
pick out 口语化；select 正式。购物/送礼场景常用 pick out。

### 从 `in for trouble` 拓展：面临困境

**课文原句：**
... we're all in for trouble.

**中文意思：**
…我们全都要有麻烦了。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| in for trouble | 要有麻烦 | We're in for trouble. |
| in big trouble | 大麻烦 | He's in big trouble. |
| in for a surprise | 要有惊喜 | You're in for a surprise. |
| headed for trouble | 走向麻烦 | That's headed for trouble. |

**总结：**
in for + 名词表"即将面临（多指坏事）"；in for a surprise 为正面用法。
