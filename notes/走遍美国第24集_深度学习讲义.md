# 📘 《走遍美国》第24集 深度学习讲义

**课文主题：** Robbie 与来自希腊的交换生 Alexandra 友谊深厚，在她返程前大家筹划惊喜送别派对；因航班取消她险些错过，最终赶回，并互赠礼物印证"友谊长存"。
**核心场景：** 厨房母子商量派对与借钱买表；Robbie 邀请 Alexandra（隐瞒惊喜）；派对夜 Alexandra 来电说要提前走，却在机场被父母劝回，惊喜揭晓。
**学习重点：** ⌒ 连读、`(schwa)` 弱读（borrow/of）、`[ ]` 失去爆破（nex(t)/las(t)）、⚡ 闪音（lit⌫le / be⚡er / par⌫y）；友情与送礼词汇；would like to、if 真实条件、反意疑问句、count on 等。

## 发音标注图例（Pronunciation Legend）

| 符号 | 名称 | 说明 | 示例 |
| ------- | -------------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| ⌒ | 连读 Linking | 前字尾辅音与后字首元音拼读，或元元连读 | `a⌒lot`, `go⌒on` |
| (schwa) | 弱读 Weak Form | 结构词元音弱化为 /ə/ 或 /ɪ/ | `t(schwa)` /tə/, `of(schwa)` /əv/ |
| `[ ]` | 失去爆破 Unreleased Stop | /p,t,k,b,d,g/ 只作发音姿势，不送气爆破 | `no[t] problem`, `don'[t]` |
| ⚡ | 闪音 Flap T/D | 两元音间 /t/、/d/ 快速弹舌，似轻快 /d/ | `wa⚡er`, `ci⚡y`, `pho⚡ographer` |

> **文本高亮约定：** **加粗** = 生词；

## 🔬 第四部分：重要语法讲解（Grammar）

### 1. would like to（委婉意愿）

**课文例句：**
Would you like to give her a little farewell party? / I'd like to give her a nice going-away present.

**结构：**
would (I'd / you'd) like to + 动词原形，表"想要/愿意"。

**用法讲解：**
比 want 更客气、更正式；常用于提议、请求与表达愿望。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| I want to... | 我想…（直接） | I want to help. |
| Do you want to...? | 你想…吗？ | Do you want to come? |
| feel like + V-ing | 想做… | I feel like resting. |

**易错点：**
would like 后接 to do，不接 doing；口语缩写为 I'd like。

**总结：**
礼貌表达"想/愿"首选 would like to，在提议与礼物场景尤其得体。

### 2. if 真实条件句

**课文例句：**
If you could lend me the money, I could pay you back out of my lifeguard salary.

**结构：**
If + 条件从句，主句用 would/could + 动词原形。

**用法讲解：**
表达有可能成立的条件及相应结果；此处 Robbie 向父亲提出假设性请求。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| If I have..., I'll... | 如果我有…就… | If I have cash, I'll buy it. |
| As long as... | 只要… | As long as you help, we're fine. |
| Suppose... | 假设… | Suppose you lend me money. |

**易错点：**
真实条件句主句用 will/would 表意愿；条件从句用现在时或 could 表委婉。

**总结：**
if 条件句用于请求、计划与假设，注意主从句时态呼应。

### 3. 反意疑问句（tag question）

**课文例句：**
You and Alexandra have become good friends, haven't you? / She's really a good friend, isn't she?

**结构：**
陈述句 + 简短助动词 + 主语（否定/肯定相反），寻求认同或确认。

**用法讲解：**
前肯后否或前否后肯；语调上扬表疑问、下降表确认。本句祖母温和确认 Robbie 的感受。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| ..., don't you? | …不是吗？ | You like her, don't you? |
| ..., will you? | …好吗？（祈使） | Close the door, will you? |
| ..., right? | …对吧？ | It's fine, right? |

**易错点：**
主语须用代词；助动词要与主句一致；nothing 等否定词作主语时，尾句用肯定。

**总结：**
反意疑问句是英语日常确认与互动的高频结构，注意前后肯定否定相反。

### 4. "have something done"（使役/被动完成）

**课文例句：**
You had it engraved!

**结构：**
have + 宾语 + 过去分词，表"让别人做某事"或"遭遇某事"。

**用法讲解：**
强调"请人完成"而非自己动手；此处指 Robbie 请人把字刻在表上。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| get sth. done | 把…办好 | I got my hair cut. |
| make sb. do | 使某人做 | She made him laugh. |
| do it myself | 自己做 | I engraved it myself. |

**易错点：**
done 是过去分词，不是原形；区别于 have（有）+ 宾语 + do（让某人做，接原形）。

**总结：**
have sth. done 表"委托他人完成"，是生活与商务中极常用结构。

### 5. when / as soon as 时间状语从句

**课文例句：**
When Alexandra arrives, we'll surprise her. / When I told them..., they insisted that I stay.

**结构：**
When/As soon as + 从句，主句表随后动作或状态。

**用法讲解：**
when 引导时间点/事件触发；主将从现（或过去时呼应）。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| as soon as | 一…就 | As soon as she comes, we start. |
| after | 在…之后 | After he left, we cleaned up. |
| once | 一旦 | Once you know them, it's easy. |

**易错点：**
when 表"当…时"；whenever 表"每当"，勿混淆。时态需主从呼应。

**总结：**
when 从句是叙事与计划的基础连接词，注意主从时态一致。

### 6. insist that + 虚拟语气（should + 动词原形）

**课文例句：**
They insisted that I stay.

**结构：**
insist that + (should) + 动词原形，表"坚持要求（某人）做"。

**用法讲解：**
insist 表"坚决要求"时，从句用虚拟语气（should 可省，直接用原形 stay）。

| 类似表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| demand that... | 要求… | He demanded that she leave. |
| suggest that... | 建议… | I suggest that he go. |
| require that... | 要求… | They require that we wait. |

**易错点：**
美式英语常省 should，直接跟原形；这是"建议/要求"类动词的共同虚拟用法。

**总结：**
insist/suggest/demand 后 that 从句用 (should) + 原形，是书面与正式口语考点。

### 从 `broke` 拓展：缺钱的表达

|---|---|---|

**总结：**
broke 最口语；short of cash 较中性；strapped 偏"周转不灵"。描述财务窘境时按需选用。

|---|---|---|

| rely on | 依靠 | We rely on his help. |
| depend on | 取决于/依赖 | It depends on the weather. |
| bank on | 指望（口语） | I'm banking on your support. |

**总结：**
count on / rely on 近义；depend on 还可表"取决于"；bank on 更口语化、带信心。

### 从 `in one's honor` 拓展：敬意与纪念

**课文原句：**
...the party is in her honor.

**中文意思：**
这个派对是为向她致敬而办。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| in one's honor | 为向…致敬 | A dinner in his honor. |
| in memory of | 为纪念… | A statue in memory of him. |
| to celebrate | 为庆祝 | We met to celebrate her. |
| on behalf of | 代表… | I speak on behalf of all. |

**总结：**
in one's honor 表"为尊重/庆祝某人"；in memory of 用于已故者，注意区分。

### 从 `borrow / loan` 拓展：借贷方向词

**课文原句：**
Maybe I could borrow some money from you and Dad. / I'll need a loan.

**中文意思：**
也许我能从你和爸那儿借点钱。/ 我需要一笔借款。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| borrow (from) | 借入 | I borrowed from my dad. |
| lend (to) | 借出 | He lent me money. |
| loan (n./v.) | 贷款/借出 | The bank loaned me cash. |
| pay back | 偿还 | I'll pay you back. |

**总结：**
borrow 是"进来"（from），lend/loan 是"出去"（to）；pay back 对应"还"。这是易混核心点。

| engrave | 雕刻（字） | She engraved his name. |
| carve | 雕刻（形） | He carved a statue. |
| emboss | 压凸纹 | The cover is embossed. |
| print | 印刷 | They printed the logo. |

**总结：**
engrave 特指"刻字/图案于硬物"；have it engraved 用 have sth. done 表委托加工。

### 从 `decorate` 拓展：装饰相关词

**课文原句：**
My mom's decorating it right now. (the cake)

**中文意思：**
我妈正在装饰它（蛋糕）。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| decorate | 装饰 | We decorated the room. |
| ornament | 点缀（n./v.） | She ornamented the tree. |
| adorn | 装点 | Flowers adorned the table. |
| set up | 布置 | They set up the party. |

**总结：**
decorate 最通用；adorn/ornament 偏书面优美；派对场景常与 set up（布置）搭配使用。

### 从 `insist` 拓展：坚持类动词与虚拟

**课文原句：**
They insisted that I stay.

**中文意思：**
他们坚持要我留下。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| insist (that) | 坚持要求 | He insisted that I go. |
| demand (that) | 要求 | She demanded that he leave. |
| urge (that) | 力劝 | They urged that we wait. |
| persist in | 坚持做（in+V-ing） | He persisted in asking. |

**总结：**
insist/demand/urge 后 that 从句用 (should) + 原形；persist 后接 in doing，结构不同。

## ❓ 第六部分：学习问题（Study Questions）

**Comprehension（理解）**
1. Why did Robbie want to give Alexandra a surprise party instead of telling her directly?（为何要办惊喜派对而非直说？）
2. What gift did Robbie plan to buy, and how did he get the money?（Robbie 打算买什么礼物？钱从哪来？）
3. Why did Alexandra almost miss the party, and how did she end up coming back?（她为何险些错过？又为何回来？）

**Thinking & Speaking（思考与口语）**
4. Have you ever had a going-away party or said goodbye to a good friend? Describe it.（你经历过送别吗？描述一次。）
5. If a friend were leaving your country, what gift would you give and why?（朋友要离开，你会送什么？为什么？）
6. Do you think surprise parties are a good idea? What could go wrong?（惊喜派对好吗？可能出什么岔子？）

**Retelling（复述）**
7. Retell Act I in 3 sentences: how Robbie and Ellen planned the party and Robbie asked Dad for a loan.（复述第一幕：筹划与借钱。）
8. Retell Act II: how Robbie invited Alexandra without revealing the surprise.（复述第二幕：隐瞒惊喜的邀请。）
9. Suppose you are Alexandra. Tell the story of your airport phone call and return in your own words.（以 Alexandra 口吻讲机场来电与折返。）
10. Use the words farewell party, borrow, wristwatch, in one's honor, engrave to retell the story in one paragraph.（用所给词一段话复述。）
