## 发音标注图例（Pronunciation Legend）

| 符号        | 名称                   | 说明                            | 示例                           |
| --------- | -------------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| ⌒         | 连读 Linking           | 前词尾音与后词首音自然连接                 | `get⌒to`, `come⌒by`          |
| `(schwa)` | 弱读 Weak Form         | 功能词弱化为 /ə/ 或 /ɪ/              | `to` /tə/, `for` /fər/       |
| `[ ]`     | 失去爆破 Unreleased Stop | /p, t, k, b, d, g/ 在辅音前不完全爆破  | `don'[t] know`, `ge[t] busy` |
| ⚡         | 闪音 Flap T/D          | 美音中两个元音之间的 /t/ 或 /d/ 变成轻快 /d/ | `bu⚡er`, `la⚡er`             |

# 🔬 第四部分：重要语法讲解（Grammar）

## 1. should 表示建议或应该做的事

**结构：**  
Subject + should + 动词原形

**课文例句：**  
We should get to work.  
I think we should take a moment and remember the meaning of Thanksgiving.

**讲解：**  
`should` 表示“应该”，语气比 `must` 柔和，常用于建议、提醒或表达合适的做法。

**扩展例句：**  
You should put on your heavy jacket.  
We should give thanks before dinner.

***

## 2. must 表示必须或强烈推测

**结构：**  
Subject + must + 动词原形

**课文例句：**  
I guess we must.  
It must be Alexandra.

**讲解：**  
`must` 可以表示“必须”，也可以表示说话人非常肯定的推测。  
`It must be Alexandra.` 表示“那一定是 Alexandra”。

**扩展例句：**  
We must finish the dinner preparations.  
That must be Susan on the phone.

***

## 3. might 表示不确定的可能性

**结构：**  
Subject + might + 动词原形

**课文例句：**  
Alexandra might call.

**讲解：**  
`might` 表示“可能，也许”，不确定性比 `will` 更强，语气更委婉。

**扩展例句：**  
She might come by later.  
Robbie might feel better after breakfast.

***

## 4. 间接引语：said + 从句

**结构：**  
Subject + said + 从句

**课文例句：**  
He said he'll call you.  
She said she'll call you later.

**讲解：**  
当转述别人说过的话时，可以使用 `said`。口语中 `that` 通常省略。

**扩展例句：**  
She said she would call back.  
He said he wanted to talk to Alexandra.

***

## 5. why don't you...? 用于提出建议

**结构：**  
Why don't you + 动词原形?

**课文例句：**  
Why don't you tell us about that, Michelle?  
Why don't you go watch the last three minutes of the game?

**讲解：**  
`Why don't you...?` 字面是“你为什么不……”，实际常用于委婉建议。

**扩展例句：**  
Why don't you sit down?  
Why don't you call her back?

***

## 6. give thanks for / be thankful for 表达感恩

**结构：**  
give thanks for + 名词或动名词  
be thankful for + 名词或动名词

**课文例句：**  
I give thanks for being here with my family.  
We have a lot to be thankful for.

**讲解：**  
这两个表达都表示“为……感恩”。`for` 后面可以接名词，也可以接动名词。

**扩展例句：**  
I give thanks for my family.  
We are thankful for this meal.

***
