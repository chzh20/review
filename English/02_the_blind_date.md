## 🔬 第四部分：重要语法讲解（Grammar）

> 语法点在正文中以 `【语法】` 标注（本集重点如下）。

### 1. would like / I'd like to do

- **课文原句：** I'd like you to meet my sister-in-law Marilyn. / I'd like to meet your daughter someday.
- **规则：** would like to do = 想要做某事（比 want 更礼貌）；would like sb. to do = 想要某人做某事。
- **例句：** I'd like to order the Mee Krob.

### 2. too ... to ...（太……而不能）

- **课文原句：** Are we too late for our dinner reservation?
- **规则：** too + adj./adv. + to do 表示「太……以至于不能……」。
- **例句：** He was too nervous to eat much.

### 3. since 引导原因状语从句

- **课文原句：** `【语法】`I think it's important for me to be there since her mother died.
- **规则：** since = because（既然；因为），说明已知原因。
- **例句：** Since you're here, let's start.

### 4. have to do（必须；不得不）

- **课文原句：** I'll have to go home. / the babysitter has to get home.
- **规则：** have to 表示客观必要性，有人称和时态变化（has to / had to）。
- **对比：** must（主观必须）vs. have to（客观需要）

### 5. keep + 宾语 + adj.

- **课文原句：** my work keeps me busy.
- **规则：** keep + sb./sth. + adj. 表示「使……保持某种状态」。
- **例句：** Keep me posted.（随时告诉我进展。）

### 6. 序数词 + date（第几次约会）

- **课文原句：** It was his first date in two years.
- **规则：** first/second date；in + 时间段表「在……时间内」。
- **例句：** It was my first visit to New York.

## ❓ 第五部分：学习问题（Study Questions）

### A. Comprehension（理解题）

1. Why is Harry late for his date with Susan?
2. What is Susan's address, and how does Harry find it?
3. Who does Susan introduce Harry to at her apartment?
4. What do Susan and Harry order at Somsak's restaurant?
5. What are Susan's and Harry's jobs?
6. Why does Harry have to leave the restaurant early?
7. What gift does Harry bring when he returns later that evening?
8. Do Susan and Harry plan to see each other again? How do you know?

### B. Thinking（思考题）

1. Harry leaves the date to care for his sick daughter. What does this tell you about his character?
2. Susan says it was Harry's first date in two years. Why might that explain his nervousness?
3. Harry and Susan both have experienced loss. How does the conversation about Harry's wife affect their connection?
4. Marilyn says "I believe it, even without looking" when Harry returns. What does she already understand about Harry?
5. Is it fair that Harry left dinner early? How did Susan handle the situation?
6. What is the difference between a "blind date" and a regular date? Why is this episode titled **The Blind Date**?

### C. Speaking & Retelling（口语与复述）

1. **Summarize** the episode in 3–4 sentences.
2. **Retell** Harry's journey from getting lost to returning with the bonsai tree.
3. **Role-play:** Harry asks a stranger for directions to 83 Wooster Street.
4. **Role-play:** Harry apologizes to Susan for leaving early. Use <u>forgive me</u> and <u>We'll make another</u>.
5. **Role-play:** Harry and Susan say good night at the door. Use <u>Have a safe trip home</u> and <u>good friends</u>.
6. Use at least three expressions from Part 3 to describe a memorable first meeting or date.
