## 🔬 第四部分：重要语法讲解（Grammar）

> 语法点在正文中以 `【语法】` 标注（本集重点如下）。

### 1. it takes time to do sth.

- **课文原句：** It just takes time to feel **comfortable** in a new place.
- **规则：** It takes + 时间 + to do，表示「做某事需要多少时间」。
- **例句：** It takes time to get used to a new city.

### 2. want / would like + sb. + to do

- **课文原句：** He doesn't want anybody picking him up. / They want me to.
- **规则：** want 后可接不定式或动名词；接人时用 want sb. to do。
- **对比：** want to do（自己想）vs. want sb. to do（想让别人做）

### 3. remind sb. of sth.

- **课文原句：** She always reminds him of Grandma.
- **规则：** remind + 人 + of + 事物，表示「使某人想起……」。
- **例句：** This song reminds me of my childhood.

### 4. instead of / because 原因从句

- **课文原句：** I **retired** because I wanted to be with my family.
- **规则：** because 引导原因状语从句，说明「为什么」做某事。
- **例句：** I moved here because I wanted to be closer to my family.

### 5. can't wait to do

- **课文原句：** <u>I can't wait to see them.</u>
- **规则：** can't wait to do = 迫不及待做某事；to 后接动词原形。
- **例句：** I can't wait to see you again.

### 6. not ... anymore

- **课文原句：** I don't feel alone anymore.
- **规则：** not ... anymore = 不再……；anymore 放句末。
- **对比：** I don't feel alone. ✓ | I don't feel alone no more. ✗（口语中偶见，书面避免双重否定）

## ❓ 第五部分：学习问题（Study Questions）

### A. Comprehension（理解题）

1. When is Grandpa expected to arrive, and how is he traveling?
2. Why doesn't the family pick Grandpa up at the station?
3. What welcome presents do Marilyn and Robbie prepare?
4. Why is Susan unhappy at the beginning of the episode?
5. What is inside Grandpa's trunk, and why can't they open it at first?
6. Where does Grandpa meet Mrs. Tobin, and what do they have in common?
7. Why did Grandpa retire and move to New York?
8. What gift does Robbie give Grandpa at the end?

### B. Thinking（思考题）

1. Grandpa says he values **independence**, but he also says he doesn't want to be alone. How do you understand this?
2. Mrs. Tobin tells Grandpa not to be so independent. Do you agree with her advice? Why or why not?
3. What role do photographs and the **family tree** play in this episode?
4. How does the conversation on the train mirror Grandpa's decision to move in with his family?
5. Have you ever felt both excited and nervous about a big change? How is Grandpa's experience similar or different?
6. What does the title **"Grandpa's Trunk"** symbolize in this story?

### C. Speaking & Retelling（口语与复述）

1. **Summarize** the episode in 3–4 sentences.
2. **Retell** the train conversation between Malcolm Stewart and Elsa Tobin in five sentences.
3. Explain the meaning of **"Small world"** and give your own example.

5. **Role-play:** Ellen welcomes Grandpa home and shows him his room. Use <u>Pleasant dreams</u> and <u>I'm sure glad you're here</u>.
