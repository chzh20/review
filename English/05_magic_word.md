## 发音标注图例（Pronunciation Legend）


| 符号        | 名称                   | 说明                            | 示例                         |
| --------- | -------------------- | ----------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| ⌒         | 连读 Linking           | 前词尾音与后词首音自然连接                 | `work⌒on`, `fall⌒out`      |
| `(schwa)` | 弱读 Weak Form         | 功能词弱化为 /ə/ 或 /ɪ/              | `to` /tə/, `for` /fər/     |
| `[ ]`     | 失去爆破 Unreleased Stop | /p, t, k, b, d, g/ 在辅音前不完全爆破  | `don'[t] want`, `ge[t] to` |
| ⚡         | 闪音 Flap T/D          | 美音中两个元音之间的 /t/ 或 /d/ 变成轻快 /d/ | `be⚡er`, `wa⚡er`           |

# 🔬 第四部分：重要语法讲解（Grammar）



## 1. be planning to do 表示“正计划做某事”

**结构：**  
Subject + be planning to + 动词原形

**课文例句：**  
Dad and I were planning to go to the game.

**讲解：**  
这个结构强调“原本有计划”。后面常接 `but`，表示计划因为某种原因发生变化。

**扩展例句：**  
We were planning to go fishing.  
She is planning to learn a new program.

---



## 2. have to 表示客观上的“必须”

**结构：**  
Subject + have to / has to + 动词原形

**课文例句：**  
He has to work today.  
First, we have to catch some fish.  
I have to go to the hospital.

**讲解：**  
`have to` 强调外部责任或现实情况造成的“不得不”。Philip 是医生，所以医院有需要时，他必须去工作。

**扩展例句：**  
I have to finish this report.  
He has to take care of his patient.

---



## 3. Maybe + 句子 表示可能或委婉建议

**结构：**  
Maybe + 主语 + 谓语

**课文例句：**  
Maybe you can teach me how to work on a computer someday.  
Maybe we can spend some time together next weekend.  
Maybe we should do it again.

**讲解：**  
`Maybe` 可以表示“不确定的可能性”，也可以让建议听起来更柔和。

**扩展例句：**  
Maybe we can go next week.  
Maybe you should talk to your father.

---



## 4. remember doing 表示“记得做过某事”

**结构：**  
remember + 动名词

**课文例句：**  
Remember catching your first fish?

**讲解：**  
`remember doing` 表示记得过去已经发生的事情。  
`remember to do` 表示记得要去做某件还没做的事。

**对比：**  
I remember catching my first fish.  
Remember to bring the fishing net.

---



## 5. in order to 表示目的

**结构：**  
in order to + 动词原形

**课文例句：**  
In order to catch fish, you have to do this.

**讲解：**  
`in order to` 表示“为了……”，比单独使用 `to` 更正式，也更强调目的。

**扩展例句：**  
In order to catch fish, you need patience.  
In order to help Albert, Philip uses his medical skills.

---



## 6. Why don't we...? 用于提出建议

**结构：**  
Why don't we + 动词原形?

**相关表达：**  
How about next Saturday?  
Do we have a date?

**讲解：**  
在本集中，Philip 用 `How about next Saturday?` 重新安排和 Robbie 的相处时间。类似地，`Why don't we...?` 也常用于提出共同活动建议。

**扩展例句：**  
Why don't we go fishing next Saturday?  
How about spending more time together?
