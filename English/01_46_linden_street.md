## 🔬 第四部分：重要语法讲解（Grammar）

> 语法点在正文中以 `【语法】` 标注（本集重点如下）。

### 1. May I ...?（礼貌请求许可）

- **课文原句：** May I take a picture of you and your little boy?
- **规则：** May I + 动词原形，比 Can I 更正式、更礼貌。
- **例句：** May I use your phone?

### 2. Would you like to ...?（礼貌邀请）

- **课文原句：** Would you like to see them? / Would you like some coffee?
- **规则：** Would you like to do / Would you like + n.，表示邀请或提议。
- **例句：** Would you like to join us for dinner?

### 3. appreciate（感谢；感激）

- **课文原句：** I appreciate your help. / I really appreciate it, Marilyn.
- **规则：** appreciate + 名词/动名词，不用 appreciate to do。
- **对比：** I appreciate your help. ✓ | I appreciate you to help. ✗

### 4. expect sb. to do / be expecting sb.

- **课文原句：** I was expecting him here at 5:45.
- **规则：** expect 可接 that 从句或 expect sb. to do；be expecting = 在等（某人）。
- **例句：** I was expecting you at six.

### 5. too + adj. + for sb.

- **课文原句：** Is this pink too bright for me?
- **规则：** too + 形容词 + for + 人，表示「对某人来说太……」。
- **例句：** This sweater is too small for me.

### 6. keep doing（一直做；反复想）

- **课文原句：** I keep thinking about that bag of film.
- **规则：** keep + doing 表示持续或反复的动作。
- **例句：** I keep forgetting his name.

## ❓ 第五部分：学习问题（Study Questions）

### A. Comprehension（理解题）

1. What is Richard Stewart's job, and what is his photo project called?
2. Where does Richard meet Alexandra, and where is she from?
3. What does Richard forget on the ferry?
4. What is the Stewart family's address?
5. How does Alexandra try to find Richard's home?
6. Why is Richard late for dinner?
7. What does Marilyn do to help find the lost bag?
8. How does Alexandra finally return the camera bag to Richard?

### B. Thinking（思考题）

1. Why does Richard want to photograph ordinary people for Family Album, U.S.A.?
2. Alexandra goes out of her way to return the bag. What does this tell you about her?
3. Marilyn calls the Lost and Found office while Richard is upset. How does she balance work and family in this episode?
4. Richard introduces Alexandra to the whole Stewart family. Why is this scene important for the series?
5. At the end, Alexandra forgets her bag again. What might this suggest about future episodes?
6. What does the address **46 Linden Street** represent in this story?

### C. Speaking & Retelling（口语与复述）

1. **Summarize** the episode in 3–4 sentences.
2. **Retell** how Richard meets Alexandra and loses his bag.
3. **Role-play:** Richard asks Mrs. Vann for permission to take a photo. Use May I...?
4. **Role-play:** Marilyn comforts Richard after he loses the film. Use Don't worry and Thanks, anyway.
5. **Role-play:** Robbie opens the door and meets Alexandra for the first time.
6. Use at least three expressions from Part 3 to thank someone who helped you.
